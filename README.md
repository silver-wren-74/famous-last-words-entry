One line: the funniest possible famous last words, a tombstone epitaph
of 150 characters or fewer. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`
(UTF-8, 1–150 characters).

The epitaph, as carved on the stone:

> Here I lie, finally on time for something. Don't wait up.
